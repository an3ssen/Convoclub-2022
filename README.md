Structured Writing: How I think it can help when conversing with an AI
===
I did a screencast on how structured writing can improve our interactions and conversations with an AI, organised by [Convoclub](https://www.convocat.nl).  


# Hello everyone, my name is Patrick Andriessen
I work as a content strategist and technical communication consultant at napnanm Publishing & Consulting.
Join me at Convoclub on the 27th of October for my talk: Structured writing: How I think it can help when conversing with an AI.
We are going to talk about what structured writing is. How I think it can improve your AI or chatbot's performance, and where you might find structured writing experts within your organization.
I'm looking forward to it and I hope to you see you all there. Cheers!
